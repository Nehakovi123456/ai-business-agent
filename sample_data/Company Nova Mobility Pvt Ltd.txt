Company: Nova Mobility Pvt Ltd

Company Information:
Nova Mobility currently manufactures 12,000 electric scooters per year.

Production:
The maximum current manufacturing capacity is 15,000 scooters per year.

Customer Research:
A survey of 2,000 customers found that 72% of customers consider price the most important factor when purchasing an electric scooter.

Target Market:
The company plans to target college students and young professionals in Tier-2 Indian cities.

Pricing:
The proposed scooter price is ₹89,999.

Marketing Budget:
The company has allocated ₹5 crore for the first-year marketing campaign.

Business Risk:
The company's biggest concern is limited manufacturing capacity.
