3. CUSTOMER SURVEY & FEEDBACK METRICS (N=4,500 Price-Sensitive Buyers)
- 78% of price-sensitive buyers prioritize battery range reliability and extended warranty over smart touchscreen features.
- 64% of respondents state an ideal price point below ₹90,000 for urban daily commute.
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) efficiency is cited as the primary purchase decision driver by 82% of respondents.

4. REGULATORY & SUBSIDY LANDSCAPE
- FAME-III / EMPS Policy Incentive provides subsidy benefits up to ₹10,000 per vehicle meeting localized battery assembly criteria.
- Local content requirement for subsidy eligibility: minimum 50% domestic sourcing.
